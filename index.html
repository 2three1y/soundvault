A keyboard-friendly directory of sound collections

Soundvault

This searchable archive brings together accessibility sounds, voices and speech synthesizers, system tones, soundpacks, and related tools. Items remain hosted by MG Storage; use the source links to browse or download. Check availability, file details, permissions, and licensing at the source.

About this archive

Sound resources can be scattered across folders and difficult to browse. Soundvault groups known files and collections in one accessible index. “Download” links point directly to a listed file; “Visit to Download” opens a source directory, especially where a collection contains multiple files or large installers.

Source archive: MG Storage.
